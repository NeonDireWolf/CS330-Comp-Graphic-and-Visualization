# CS330-Comp-Graphic-and-Visualization

1. How do I approach designing software?
What new design skills has your work on the project helped you to craft?

This project improved my ability to break a visual concept into smaller, manageable components. I learned to design scenes by identifying individual objects, their transformations, and their rendering requirements rather than treating the scene as a single task.

What design process did you follow for your project work?

I followed an iterative design process that began with understanding the project requirements and planning the scene layout. I implemented objects incrementally, testing positioning, textures, and lighting as each component was added, which allowed me to refine the design based on visual feedback.

How could tactics from your design approach be applied in future work?

Breaking problems into components and designing iteratively can be applied to future projects of any type. This approach helps reduce complexity, improve maintainability, and catch issues early.

2. How do I approach developing programs?
What new development strategies did you use while working on your 3D scene?

I used incremental development and frequent testing while building the scene. Adding features in small steps made debugging easier, especially when working with OpenGL state, shaders, and lighting.

How did iteration factor into your development?

Iteration was essential throughout the project. Each milestone built upon the previous one, allowing me to refine object placement, lighting behavior, and textures until the scene matched the intended design.

How has your approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones?

Over time, my focus shifted from simply rendering objects to writing more structured, organized code. I became more deliberate about reusing existing systems and aligning my implementation closely with the assignment requirements.

3. How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?
How do computational graphics and visualizations help in your future educational pathway?

This course strengthened my understanding of linear algebra, coordinate systems, and how mathematical concepts translate into visual output. These skills support future coursework involving graphics, simulations, and data visualization.

How do computational graphics and visualizations help in your future professional pathway?

Professionally, this project improved my problem-solving and debugging skills while working with complex systems. Experience with rendering pipelines, shaders, and iterative development is valuable in fields such as game development, simulation, and visualization software.
